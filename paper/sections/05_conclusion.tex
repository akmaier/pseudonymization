\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

Protecting the image does not mitigate privacy leakage from a report that still contains patient identifiers. We evaluate detection, downstream utility, and re-identification risk on the corresponding text. A large detector union replaces nearly all person mentions and keeps both attacks near chance. Utility depends more on detector precision than on replacement with surrogates or placeholders, while mention-level sensitivity can underestimate exposure across documents, cases, and languages. We will release the code upon acceptance.
