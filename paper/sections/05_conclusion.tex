\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

For text we priced detection, utility and re-identification on the same documents. A large union
ensemble replaces almost every person mention and holds both attacks near their chance rates.
Detector precision governs utility more than the choice between surrogates and placeholders, and
sensitivity understates exposure per document, case and language. There is no single anonymisation
score: a release should state its operating point, per-language errors, surviving cases, attacker
knowledge and stability.
