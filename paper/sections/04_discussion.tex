\section{Discussion}\label{sec:discussion}

Four recommendations follow, and one prediction does not survive.

\textbf{Prefer a large union to a consensus rule, and price it.} Agreement buys specificity, 0.869
against 0.986 on CARDIO:DE, and pays in sensitivity, which every leakage measure follows: two votes
on Enron take person sensitivity from 0.890 to 0.732 and raise fixed linkage fivefold.
\textbf{State the attacker's knowledge with the number.} A corpus-internal prior is an upper bound,
not a risk, and here the two differ by the whole result. \textbf{Report the denominator you release
on.} No corpus-level score describes a document, a case or a language.

\textbf{Choose the replacement form for utility, not for privacy.} The placeholder release is a
rewrite of the surrogate release, so neither is safer against an adversary who knows the scheme, and
merely telling the ranker so lifts it from 0.110 to 0.165 on unchanged text. The surrogate's real
advantage is that token-level tasks survive it and do not survive \texttt{[PERSON]}; at document
level the placeholder is better.

The prediction that sensitivity moves the two attacks in opposite directions is refuted. Better
detection was expected to starve context linkage while leaving a larger population for frequency
alignment. Instead alignment recovered nothing at any sensitivity and both linkage attacks fell as
it rose: both are bounded by the identifiers the detector missed.

The limits are specific. Linkage is measurable only on Enron and CARDIO:DE, because TAB and
OntoNotes link mentions within a document; people recur there, so those figures understate rather
than bound the risk. CARDIO:DE names were inserted into an already de-identified release, making its
frequency distribution synthetic and its clinical realism partial. Dates pass through by design, a
channel this pipeline does not close. The candidate-ranking attack presents ten names, bounding an
adversary with a shortlist rather than one searching a population. Every number describes one
release under one key.
